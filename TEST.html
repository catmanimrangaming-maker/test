About VCET Asia

Your One-Stop Solution for Mechanical Components

Established in 2004 in Malacca, VCET Asia bridges Swiss engineering expertise with high-capacity manufacturing ecosystems across Malaysia and Southeast Asia.

High Precision Machining

Manufacturing prototype, single-unit, and mass-production high-precision mechanical parts with micron-level accuracy.

Swiss Quality Assurance

Internal QA/QC labs equipped with vision (MMV) and 3D coordinate measuring machines (MMT) to meet demanding European criteria.

Network Capacity

Over 20 audited partner manufacturing plants providing access to 600+ advanced CNC, EDM, and grinding machines.

Our History

Two decades of continuous growth and technological advancement.

Facility Infrastructure

  • Modern Reception & Admin Offices
  • Dedicated Conference Rooms
  • Specialized QC & Assembly Rooms
  • Optimized Logistics Zone

2004 - 2010

Creation of the enterprise, establishment of the first VCET Asia office, setup of the Quality Control department, and commencement of mechanical subcontracting and assembly activities.

2011 - 2015

Setup of the Project Management team, QA/QC departments, Finance, and Logistics. Acquisition of the first MMV machine. Implementation of the first ERP system (March 1, 2014).

2016 - 2019

Upgrade of the ERP system to a modern cloud-based infrastructure for total operational integration.

2020 - 2024

Relocation to the new building. Expansion of QA capabilities with the purchase of advanced MMV and MMT (Coordinate Measuring) machines.
